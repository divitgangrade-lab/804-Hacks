The Pareto Trade-off Frontier

01 · INTAKE THERMODYNAMICS

Reading Ashburn's Air

Cooling towers don't care about ambient thermometer temperature. They care about wet-bulb temperature — the temperature water reaches by evaporating. Below 10°C wet-bulb, dry economizers run with zero water. Above 22°C, sprayers gulp gallons just to keep GPU clusters below thermal limits.

02 · THE 66% CLIMATE PARADOX

Why Carbon-Only Fails

Most schedulers optimize for carbon alone. Big mistake. In Virginia, 66% of the time, PJM's cleanest solar/nuclear hours hit around 1 to 2 PM — the exact hottest hour of the afternoon when cooling towers evaporate peak water. Chasing carbon blindly can actually worsen local watershed drought.

03 · PRAGMATIC SCHEDULING

Batch Compute Doesn't Mind Waiting

Your team doesn't need checkpoint evaluations or bulk fine-tuning runs to finish at 3:15 PM instead of 11:30 PM. Waiting just 4 to 6 hours for nighttime cool air trims ~24% off on-site water consumption without buying new chillers or dropping GPU clocks.
