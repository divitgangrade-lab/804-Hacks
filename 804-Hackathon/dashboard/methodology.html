Pillar 01 · Psychrometrics

Wet-Bulb Thermodynamics

Evaporative cooling towers reject server heat by evaporating water into outside air. Water demand depends on wet-bulb temperature (Twb), not dry-bulb temperature alone.

Twb = T · atan(0.151977 · (RH% + 8.313659)^0.5)
+ atan(T + RH%) - atan(RH% - 1.676331)
+ 0.00391838 · RH%^1.5 · atan(0.023101 · RH%)
- 4.686035 [1]

In this model, Twb ≤ 10°C selects dry economizer mode with 0.0 L/kWh on-site cooling water [4].

Source Notes · 01

Psychrometrics

  • Stull (2011), wet-bulb temperature from relative humidity and air temperature. The approximation is intended for ordinary near-surface conditions (−20 to 50°C; 5–99% RH).
  • The 10°C switch point and linear WUE curve are implementation assumptions, not a universal data-center operating standard. Facilities should replace them with site-specific controls and measured water data.
Pillar 02 · Grid Coupling

PJM Generation Mix

Hourly PJM generation by fuel type comes from EIA Form 930 [2]. It is a generation mix, not a measurement of marginal dispatch.

Nuclear: 2.54 L/kWh
Coal: 2.60 L/kWh
Natural Gas CC: 0.75 L/kWh
Solar & Wind: ~0.00 L/kWh
[3]

The optimizer combines estimated upstream generation water with on-site cooling water.

Grid Coupling

  • Macknick et al. (2012), operational water consumption and withdrawal factors for electricity-generating technologies. Factors vary by plant and cooling system; these are representative model inputs, not PJM real-time measurements.
Pillar 03 · Hardware Protocol

Rack Unit Telemetry

A custom serial protocol connects the dashboard through Web Serial at 115200 baud to a 16×2 LCD mirror and PWM fan driver:

Frame: WBLB:[Twb]:[WUE]:[FAN_PWM]\n
Example: WBLB:14.2:0.63:180
Joystick: debounced GPIO interrupt
Safety: immediate thermal override

The protocol is designed for Arduino Uno/Nano, Raspberry Pi Pico, and ESP32 targets.

Source Notes · 03

  • Each board requires compatible firmware and a supported serial connection; this is not plug-and-play without that setup.
