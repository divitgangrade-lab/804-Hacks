Pillar 01 · Psychrometrics

Wet-Bulb Thermodynamics

Evaporative cooling towers reject server heat by evaporating water into the outside air. The rate of water consumption is bounded not by ambient dry-bulb temperature, but by wet-bulb temperature (Twb).

Twb = T · atan(0.151977 · (RH% + 8.313659)^0.5)
+ atan(T + RH%) - atan(RH% - 1.676331)
+ 0.00391838 · RH%^1.5 · atan(0.023101 · RH%)
- 4.686035 (Stull 2011)

At Twb ≤ 10°C, data centers switch to 100% dry free air economizers, evaporating 0.0 L/kWh.

Pillar 02 · Grid Coupling

PJM Marginal Fuel Mix

Thermal power plants also consume massive water off-site for turbine condensers. We ingest real-time PJM generation by fuel type from EIA-930:

Nuclear: 2.54 L/kWh (Macknick 2012)
Coal: 2.60 L/kWh (condenser cooling)
Natural Gas CC: 0.75 L/kWh
Solar & Wind: ~0.00 L/kWh

Our Pareto optimizer accounts for both on-site cooling tower water and upstream power-plant water withdrawal.

Pillar 03 · Hardware Protocol

Rack Unit Telemetry

Physical data center integration utilizes a custom serial daemon connected via WebSerial (115200 baud) to a 16x2 LCD mirror and PWM fan driver on each server rack:

Frame Format: `WBLB:[Twb]:[WUE]:[FAN_PWM]\n`
Example: `WBLB:14.2:0.63:180`
Joystick: Debounced GPIO interrupt
Safety: Immediate thermal override

Works out-of-the-box with Arduino Uno/Nano, Raspberry Pi Pico, and ESP32 microcontrollers.
